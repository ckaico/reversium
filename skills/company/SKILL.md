# Company method

Goal: how the company moves people from first touch to repeat purchase, from what anyone can see.

Public surfaces only: website, pricing page, public changelog, ads library, public filings, public social. Nothing behind a login, no guessing at private numbers; where you estimate, say so.

Sections:
- **TARGET** — the company, what it sells, stated scale if public.
- **REACH** — channels actually used, cadence, paid vs. earned (ads library tells you).
- **OFFER** — tiers, prices, trial shape, the upgrade moment.
- **RETAIN** — what keeps people: cadence of releases, community, support posture, refund policy.
- **FOUND** — the one dependency the whole system rests on.
- **PLAN** — the user's version: one channel, one price, one weekly artifact. Drawn as a diagram in text.
- **LOOP** — metric the user will track weekly.
- **RULES** — public signals only · estimates marked · no private data.
