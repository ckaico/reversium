# Acceptable use

Version 0.1 · 2026-11-08 · Draft, in force for this repository. Not legal advice.

Reversium is for understanding how something you have lawful access to was made, and building your own version from that understanding. The server enforces the parts it can (an attestation on every blueprint, ownership proof before any site read) and refuses by naming the clause below.

## You may
1. Analyse devices you own, software you have licensed, media you can lawfully watch or listen to, and pages that are public without logging in.
2. Study methods, structures, styles, timings, protocols and formats. These are ideas and techniques, not protected expression.
3. Build interoperable tools for your own hardware and software, where the law in your country allows it.
4. Request a security read of a website you control, after proving ownership by DNS record or verification file.

## You may not
1. Break or work around access controls, licence checks, DRM or encryption. Reversium will not help with this and ships no tooling for it.
2. Modify or disable safety systems, flight-restriction systems or regulatory limits on any device.
3. Log in to platforms through Reversium, automate accounts, or collect data from behind logins. Paste links. Use your own exports.
4. Upload or analyse material you do not have the right to: leaked files, material under NDA, someone else's private data.
5. Reproduce protected expression as output: full lyrics, full transcripts, note-for-note transcriptions, clips, copies of a work.
6. Point a security read at a site you do not control, or use any part of Reversium to load, probe or disrupt someone else's systems.
7. Redistribute vendor firmware, binaries or assets obtained during analysis.

## Your data
Reversium is local-first. Blueprints, loop rounds and task files live in `./.reversium/` in your project and are not sent anywhere by this software. `teardown_render` writes a static page to `./teardown/`; publishing it is your action.
